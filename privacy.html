本隱私權政策說明「隱私保險箱」（以下稱「本 App」）如何處理你的資訊。本 App 由個人開發者 程泓凱（以下稱「開發者」或「我」）開發與營運。

重點摘要
  • 你存放在保險箱中的帳號密碼、筆記、照片、影片、錄音與文件，只存在你的手機裡，並以高強度加密保護。
  • 開發者沒有任何伺服器儲存你的保險箱內容，也無法解密、查看或替你找回資料。
  • 你可以選擇把加密後的備份存到你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟；Google 與開發者都無法讀取其內容。
  • 本 App 顯示 Google AdMob 廣告，廣告服務會使用廣告 ID 等裝置資訊，但完全無法存取保險箱內容。
  • 我們不販售任何個人資料。

1. 保險箱內容（只在你的手機上）

你新增的帳號密碼、私密筆記、照片、影片、錄音與文件，皆以 XChaCha20-Poly1305 加密後儲存在手機的 App 私有空間。主密碼經 Argon2id 運算轉換為金鑰，主密碼與 24 個單字的復原金鑰都不會離開你的手機，也不會傳送給開發者。

因為採用「零知識」設計，開發者無法存取你的資料；若你同時遺失主密碼與復原金鑰，任何人（包括開發者）都無法還原資料。

在 App 內拍攝的照片會在加密前移除 GPS 位置等中繼資料；匯入的 JPEG 照片也會移除這些資訊。

2. Google 雲端硬碟備份（選用）

若你開啟雲端備份，本 App 會請你登入 Google 帳號，並只要求 drive.file 權限：本 App 只能存取它自己建立的檔案，無法看到你雲端硬碟中的其他檔案。

  • 上傳的內容全部是已加密的保險箱檔案，以及一份說明用的文字檔（README）。
  • 備份直接從你的手機傳送到 Google，不經過開發者的任何伺服器。
  • 你的 Google 帳號 Email 只會儲存在你的手機上，用來顯示備份狀態。
  • 你可以隨時在 App 設定中關閉備份，或到 Google 雲端硬碟刪除「隱私保險箱備份」資料夾，並可在 Google 帳戶權限頁面撤銷本 App 的存取權。

本 App 使用與傳輸自 Google API 取得的資訊，均遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」（Limited Use）規定。

3. 緊急聯絡人（付費功能，選用）

你可以指定家人在緊急時開啟保險箱。設定時你會輸入家人的姓名、身分證字號、健保卡卡號後 4 碼，以及你自訂的問題與答案。

  • 身分證字號、健保卡後 4 碼與答案不會被儲存，只在你的手機上用來運算出一把加密金鑰，運算後即丟棄。
  • 家人的姓名與你設定的問題會儲存在保險箱檔案中（也包含在你的加密雲端備份中），以便家人在開啟時選擇自己的名字並看到問題。
  • 緊急開啟的時間與聯絡人姓名會記錄在你的手機上，供你下次解鎖時查看。
  • 開發者不會收到上述任何資料。請你在設定前，依法徵得家人的同意或告知家人。

5. 廣告（Google AdMob）

本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（清單中的橫幅與原生廣告、解鎖後的開啟廣告，以及你自願觀看的獎勵影片）。AdMob 可能收集並使用以下資訊來提供、衡量與個人化廣告：

  • 廣告 ID（Android Advertising ID）
  • 裝置與系統資訊（例如機型、作業系統版本、語言）
  • IP 位址與由此推估的大略位置
  • 廣告的顯示與點擊紀錄

廣告服務無法存取保險箱中的任何內容，家屬緊急開啟時也不會顯示廣告。你可以在 App「設定 → 廣告」關閉個人化廣告，或在手機系統設定中重設或刪除廣告 ID。位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士的使用者，會先看到同意聲明，可選擇是否同意。

詳情請參考 Google 隱私權政策及 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

6. 付費購買

「永久移除廣告」與「緊急聯絡人名額」由 Google Play 處理付款。開發者不會取得你的信用卡或付款資訊；Google Play Console 會提供開發者訂單紀錄（例如商品、訂單編號與金額），用於處理退款與客服。

購買的緊急聯絡人名額與對應的訂單編號，會記錄在你手機上的保險箱檔案中，並隨你的加密備份或換機直傳一起搬移，不會傳送給開發者。

換機直傳（Wi-Fi）

換手機時，你可以讓兩支手機在同一個 Wi-Fi 內直接傳送保險箱。資料只在兩支手機之間傳輸，並以一次性金鑰再加密一層，不經過任何網路伺服器，開發者也收不到。

意見箱與評分

「意見箱」會開啟你手機的 Email App，由你自行寄出。開發者會收到你的 Email 地址、你填寫的內容，以及你選擇附上的 App 版本與系統版本，只用於回覆與改善 App。App 內的評分視窗由 Google Play 提供，開發者無法得知你是否評分或給了幾顆星。

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Private Vault" app (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed and operated by the individual developer CHENG HUNG KAI ("the developer", "we", "I").

Summary
  • The logins, notes, photos, videos, audio and files you keep in the vault stay on your phone and are protected with strong encryption.
  • The developer has no server that stores your vault content and cannot decrypt, view or recover your data.
  • You can choose to store an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive. Neither Google nor the developer can read its content.
  • The App shows Google AdMob ads. The ad service uses device information such as the advertising ID but cannot access your vault content.
  • We do not sell any personal data.

1. Vault content (on your phone only)

Logins, notes, photos, videos, audio recordings and files you add are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 and stored in the App's private storage on your phone. Your master password is turned into a key with Argon2id. Your master password and your 24-word recovery key never leave your phone and are never sent to the developer.

Because of this zero-knowledge design, the developer cannot access your data. If you lose both your master password and your recovery key, nobody, including the developer, can recover your data.

Photos taken in the App have GPS location and other metadata removed before encryption; imported JPEG photos have this metadata removed as well.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on cloud backup, the App asks you to sign in with Google and requests only the drive.file permission: the App can only access files it created and cannot see any other files in your Drive.

  • Everything uploaded is encrypted vault files, plus one explanatory text file (README).
  • Backups go directly from your phone to Google and never pass through any server operated by the developer.
  • Your Google account email is stored only on your phone, to show backup status.
  • You can turn off backup in the App's settings at any time, delete the "Private Vault Backup" folder in Google Drive, and revoke the App's access on your Google Account permissions page.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Emergency contacts (optional paid feature)

You can name family members who may open the vault in an emergency. When setting this up, you enter the family member's name, national ID number, the last 4 digits of their NHI card, and a question and answer of your choice.

  • The national ID number, NHI digits and answer are not stored. They are used only on your phone to derive an encryption key and are then discarded.
  • The family member's name and the question are stored in the vault file (and in your encrypted cloud backup) so they can choose their name and see the question when opening the vault.
  • The time of each emergency opening and the contact's name are recorded on your phone so you can see them the next time you unlock.
  • None of this is sent to the developer. Please obtain your family member's consent or inform them as required by law before setting this up.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads (banner and native ads in the item list, an app-open ad after unlocking, and rewarded videos you choose to watch). AdMob may collect and use the following to serve, measure and personalize ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and system information (such as model, OS version and language)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad impressions and clicks

The ad service cannot access anything in your vault, and no ads are shown when a family member uses emergency access. You can turn off personalized ads in the App under Settings → Ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland are first shown a consent message and can choose whether to consent.

For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Purchases

Payments for "Remove ads forever" and "Emergency contact slots" are processed by Google Play. The developer never receives your card or payment details; Google Play Console gives the developer order records (such as the product, order number and amount) for refunds and support.

Purchased emergency contact slots and their order numbers are recorded in the vault file on your phone and move with your encrypted backup or Wi-Fi transfer. They are not sent to the developer.

Wi-Fi transfer

When switching phones, you can send your vault directly between two phones on the same Wi-Fi. Data travels only between the two phones, encrypted again with a one-time key, and never passes through any internet server or reaches the developer.

Feedback and ratings

"Feedback" opens your phone's email app and you send the message yourself. The developer receives your email address, what you wrote and, if you choose, the app and system version, and uses them only to reply and improve the App. The in-app rating card is provided by Google Play; the developer cannot see whether you rated the App or how many stars you gave.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools to analyze how you use the App.
  • We do not read files, photos or contacts you have not chosen.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: uninstalling the App deletes all vault data on your phone.
  • Cloud backup: delete the "Private Vault Backup" folder in your Google Drive.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; see the Google Privacy Policy for how Google retains ad data.

Because the developer does not hold your vault content, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Security

We protect your data with widely recognized encryption (XChaCha20-Poly1305, Argon2id, X25519), but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure. Please keep your master password and recovery key safe and keep your phone's software up to date.
